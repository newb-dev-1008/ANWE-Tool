Key Limitations & Caveats
All must be considered before using tool outputs

    🐾 Animal Welfare Context

    Potential effects on food, water, shelter, breeding and movement for each focal species/group. Evidence types: Observation Inference Unknown

    ℹ️
    Interpretation guide: Welfare concerns are assessed across five domains (food, water, shelter, breeding, movement). Evidence types distinguish direct observation, informed inference from ecological principles, and unknown gaps. Absence of a record does NOT confirm species absence. Click any species card or welfare cell for detail.

    ⚖️ Compare Recovery Pathways

    Three plausible options — benefits, harms, affected stakeholders, evidence, uncertainty and field checks

    ⚠️
    No overall score is shown. Displaying a single composite score would conceal severe harm to smaller or less-represented species groups. Review each option's per-species welfare impacts and ethics flags before advising a decision.

    📝 Expert Review & Decision Record

    Editable notes · Challenge / override fields · Unresolved risks · Human decision rationale

    🧑‍💼
    Human decision authority: This tool does NOT automatically issue or execute management instructions. All outputs require expert review. Use this screen to record challenges, overrides, unresolved concerns and a rationale. The completed record can be exported for audit purposes.

    Records are saved only in this browser. Reviewer qualifications are not verified; this is not official approval.

    ⚖️ Multispecies Justice Analysis

    Who may benefit? Who may be harmed? Whose interests are missing or underweighted?

    ℹ️
    Multispecies Justice asks us to look beyond charismatic flagship species and consider the full range of affected beings — including invertebrates, soil biota and species not yet surveyed. A high "risk of omission" flag means these groups are at risk of being systematically excluded from planning, not that they are necessarily absent.
