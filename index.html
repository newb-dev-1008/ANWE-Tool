    🔍 Reviewer Disagreements / Overrides

    What do these terms mean?

    NDVI — Normalized Difference Vegetation Index: a satellite indicator of vegetation greenness. Higher values often mean more green vegetation; they do not directly measure ecological recovery or animal wellbeing.

    NBR — Normalized Burn Ratio: an index used to investigate burned land and vegetation change.

    dNBR — differenced NBR: before-image NBR minus after-image NBR. Positive values mean NBR decreased. A value of 0.1 does not mean 10% damage. This demo does not assign validated severity classes.

    Sentinel-2: the Copernicus satellite mission supplying imagery. Previews are overview images; calculated layers are grids of pixel measurements. Masked/blank pixels mean excluded or missing data.

    CEMS: Copernicus Emergency Management Service, the source of the displayed fire extent. EFFIS: European Forest Fire Information System; its historical severity data are unavailable here.

    Mean: the average of valid pixels in the regional window, including unburned land. It does not describe every location.

    Ecological evidence

    Observed means a source record exists. Derived means a documented calculation. Neither establishes present animals or measured welfare.

    Understanding GBIF and the comparison

    GBIF — Global Biodiversity Information Facility: a network and index of biodiversity records from many contributors.

    Occurrence record: a taxon reported at a place and time, not a count of all animals. Distinct species: different identified species in the records, counting each once.

    Buffer: a comparison zone outside the mapped burned extent (default 5 km). Uncertain location: a record whose location precision is unknown or may cross a boundary.

    Groups: Aves = birds; Mammalia = mammals; Amphibia = amphibians; Insecta = insects; Arachnida = spiders and relatives; Squamata = lizards and snakes.

    More records can mean more observation effort. No records does not prove absence. The PDF includes a fuller plain-language glossary.
